About

Data people can act on.

I'm a data analyst completing an MSc in Computer Science (Climate Change), where I study how AI and machine learning can be applied to climate problems. I take messy data, from satellite imagery and climate models to surveys and operational records, and turn it into analysis anyone can use. Most of my work so far is on climate and the environment in Zambia, and the skills behind it carry to any sector: cleaning and validating data, statistical testing, machine learning, spatial analysis, dashboards and clear reporting.

Approach

Careful methods, plain results.

I start with the question someone needs answered, check the data before I trust it, and test whether a pattern is real before I report it. Then I write it up for whoever has to make the decision, with the uncertainty stated honestly. I work in Python, R and SQL, and build dashboards in Power BI, Tableau or the browser depending on who will use them.
