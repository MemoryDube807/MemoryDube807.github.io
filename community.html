Stories

Community initiatives that matter

Education outreach

Pad Drive for High School Girls

This initiative provides sanitary pads and menstrual hygiene products to young girls in local high schools. The goal is simple but powerful: remove barriers that keep girls from attending school consistently and give them the dignity and support they deserve.

Beyond supplies, the project promotes awareness, confidence, and continued access to education, especially for learners facing financial and social pressure.

Environmental action

Campus Tree Planting Initiatives

Through collaboration with environmental departments, student groups, and local communities, this initiative focuses on tree planting and climate awareness. It encourages a practical connection between environmental science and everyday action.

These efforts help build stronger awareness around sustainability while creating hands-on opportunities for young people to participate in climate resilience work.
